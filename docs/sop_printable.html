1. Document Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational, administrative, compliance, and release guidelines governing the Connectzy Construction & Building Materials Platform. Connectzy bridges property developers, homeowners, civil builders, MEP engineers, interior contractors, and raw material suppliers under a verified and transparent digital marketplace.

All operational staff, platform moderators, technical leads, and customer support representatives must follow these procedures to maintain data integrity, legal compliance, and customer satisfaction.

4. Operational Standard Operating Procedures

SOP-01: Provider Onboarding, KYC Verification & Status Administration

Operational Goal
Guarantee zero fraudulent contractors on the Connectzy network while maintaining a rapid < 4-hour approval turnaround.
1. Registration Provider completes 4-step registration on mobile app
→
2. Pending Screen Session locked on VerificationPendingScreen
→
3. Admin Review Admin checks Aadhar, PAN & portfolio images
→
4. Live Activation Approval triggers FCM push & instant dashboard unlock

Standard Operating Steps:

  1. Intake Verification: Admin accesses /admin?view=pending. Click Review Application to inspect uploaded credentials.
  2. Identity Document Checklist:
    • Verify Aadhar number validity and ensure full name matches ownerName.
    • Validate PAN format (10 alphanumeric characters). Ensure business entity name corresponds to registration.
    • Audit portfolio photographs: Verify original construction site images without watermarks from third-party websites.
  3. Approval Decision:
    • Approve: Click Approve Provider. Database sets isVerified=true, dispatches an approval email via SMTP, and transmits an FCM push notification. The mobile app automatically navigates the provider to the dashboard.
    • Reject: If unreadable or fraudulent, click Reject Provider. Record explicit reason (e.g., "PAN image blurred; re-upload legible copy"). The provider sees this explanation with direct dialer to +91 81225 58115.
  4. Enforcing Real-Time Account Deactivation:
    • If an approved contractor incurs complaints, Admin toggles the switch on Approved Providers list.
    • The backend flags isVerified=false.
    • The mobile app's 15-second heartbeat listener and background FCM message automatically intercept active sessions, redirecting the user immediately to the Login Approval Pending screen.

SOP-02: Project Creation, Bidding & Milestone Execution

1. Client Posts Project Specifies budget, timeline & location
→
2. Matching & Quotes Verified contractors submit estimates
→
3. Agreement Lock Client accepts quote; contract binds
→
4. Tasks & Handover Tasks completed (100%) & client signs off

Standard Operating Steps:

  1. Posting Requirement: Client launches /create-project, entering title, estimated budget, construction stage, and service category (e.g., Builders, Architecture, Interiors, LED Walls & Digital Displays).
  2. Bidding Period: Contractors in that category review the lead. Quotations include:
    • Total estimated cost (₹).
    • Detailed milestone scope of work & planned completion date.
  3. Acceptance: Client accepts the optimal proposal. Quote transitions to isAccepted: true.
  4. Milestone Execution:
    • Contractor logs daily progress under Provider Job Detail.
    • Tasks progress through Not Started → In Progress → Completed.
    • Overall project percentage dynamically updates on both Client and Provider dashboards.
  5. Handover & Archive: When all tasks reach 100%, client inspects the premises and triggers Accept Final Handover. Project status becomes Completed.

SOP-03: B2B Materials Procurement & Purchase History

Procurement Guarantee
All B2B orders undergo closed-deal receipt tracking. Completed deliveries automatically archive to the client's permanent Purchased Materials History.

Standard Operating Steps:

  1. Supplier Catalog Upload: Suppliers add items at /supplier-product-management, detailing unit price, unit type (Bags, Tons, Units, Sq Ft), and baseline Cost of Goods Sold (costPrice).
  2. Buyer RFQ: Buyer browses /b2b-products and submits inquiry with quantity and delivery site location.
  3. Quote Negotiation (Tab 0 - Quotes / Inquiries): Supplier sets quotation rate per unit and applicable GST (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).
  4. Shipment Tracking (Tab 1 - In Transit): When buyer accepts, order moves to In Transit. Supplier records vehicle dispatch numbers and logistics updates.
  5. Delivery Finalization (Tab 2 - Purchased History):
    • Upon physical offloading at site, supplier selects Finalize & Close Deal and sets delivery status to Delivered.
    • Order status updates to Closed / Completed.
    • Buyer immediately sees the order in Purchased Materials History (accessible via Profile or /b2b-materials?tab=2) with invoice breakdown and verified green badge.

Service Project Profits
Tracks accepted contractor quotes, project volume & contractor earnings
Material Order Profits
Tracks supplier accounts, gross sales volume, COGS & net platform margins

Profit Calculation Formulas:

Stream Gross Revenue Cost of Goods (COGS) Net Profit / Margin Formula
Material Orders (With COGS) Unit Quoted Price × Qty Product costPrice × Qty Gross Revenue - COGS
Material Orders (Default Margin) Unit Quoted Price × Qty Not Provided (0.00) Gross Revenue × 10% (Platform Fee)
Service Projects Accepted Quote Cost Direct Contractor Execution Platform commission on project billing

Reconciliation Steps:

  1. Navigate to Admin Console → Earnings & Profits.
  2. Select sub-tab Material Profits (By Account) to inspect supplier-specific volume.
  3. Filter by period (Day, Month, Year) to generate periodic financial summaries.
  4. Click an individual supplier account in the leaderboard to isolate that supplier's itemized transaction ledger.

5. Technical SOP: Build, Versioning & Release Management

SOP-06: Production Android App Bundle (.aab) Build Protocol

Environment Requirement
Release builds must execute under Microsoft OpenJDK 17 and Android SDK Command-line Tools to ensure debug symbols strip cleanly.

Standard Build Sequence:

  1. Static Analysis Verification: 
    & "C:\Users\massa\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" analyze
    Build only when analyzer returns: No issues found!
  2. Update Version Specification: Open pubspec.yaml and increment the version at line 19: 
    version: 1.0.X+Y   # Example: version: 1.0.2+6 (versionCode 6, versionName 1.0.2)
    Note: Google Play Console will reject bundles with duplicate or lower version codes.
  3. Build Production AAB: 
    & "C:\Users\massa\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" build appbundle --release
  4. Output Verification: Confirm generated bundle at: 
    D:\construction app\build\app\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab
    Verify file size is approximately 72MB – 76MB.

SOP-07: Google Play Console Release & Compliance Submission

  1. Log in to Google Play Console → Select Connectzy app.
  2. Navigate to Release → Production → Create New Release.
  3. Upload app-release.aab.
  4. Trader Status Declaration: Under Policy & Compliance, when prompted "Does your trader status apply to this app?":
    ✔ Select: "Yes, my trader status applies to this app" (mandatory under European Union Digital Services Act).
  5. Release Notes (Copy & Paste): 
    What's New in Version 1.0.2:
• LED Walls & Digital Displays: Discover and hire verified LED display specialists in the Marketplace.
• Real-time Service Search: Quickly find 50+ construction & specialty services with instant search & filters.
• Purchased Materials History: Track past delivered material orders, receipts & order status in your Account.
• Performance & Stability: Enhanced order tracking, faster transitions & bug fixes.
  6. Click Save → Review Release → Start Rollout to Production.
