7. Step-by-Step Onboarding Walkthrough

Client / Homeowner Flow
  1. Sign Up: Register with your mobile number & enter OTP.
  2. Post Project: Specify work category, plot size, estimated budget, city, and upload reference blueprints or photos.
  3. Review Bids: Compare itemized proposals submitted by local verified contractors.
  4. Award & Build: Approve quotation, set milestone stages, track live progress with verified site photos.
  5. Shop Materials: Order wholesale cement, steel, paints, or LED services anytime.
Contractor & Specialist Partner Flow
  1. Register Business: Submit business name, owner name, trade category, and GST/Aadhaar.
  2. Admin KYC Approval: Team verifies business credentials within 2–4 hours.
  3. Activate Membership: Subscribe to the ₹5,999/yr unlimited membership plan.
  4. Browse & Bid: Filter live project leads by distance and category; submit quotes.
  5. Connect & Close: Call clients directly, finalize site visits, and execute milestones.
